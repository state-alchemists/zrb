- **Breaking: six voice settings retired, and the small model answers what a stop asks** (`src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_dictation.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/feature.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/words.py`, `src/zrb/config/retired.py`, ADR-0105, ADR-0076): `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_ACTION` and the `_TURN_END_TIMEOUT` it alone used are gone — anything said over zrb steers the turn, and a stop cancels it, so there is no `cancel` left to configure. `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_ECHO_COOLDOWN`, `_SELF_ECHO_MATCH` and `_SELF_ECHO_TAIL` are gone: the transcript-level guards for zrb's own voice were a patch for an audio-level problem, they only ever caught the echo that had already cleared the loudness bar, and what they caught is what `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_MIN_WORDS` and the judge answer anyway. `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_TRAILING_WORDS` is gone: an utterance ends after `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_MIN_SILENCE` once it has words, with no end-of-turn word list. `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_POLITE_WORDS` narrows to approvals — a yes or a no may carry one ("yes please", "no thanks"), a stop may not, so "stop please" is put to the small model rather than matched. - **Fix: barge-in holds zrb's voice the moment it is talked over, and the words decide whether it stays held** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/feature.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/interrupt_judge.py`, `src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/barge_in.md`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_dictation.py`, ADR-0105): the hold was taken on the words, so with a transcriber that finishes a sentence at a time — `gpt-4o-transcribe`, or any batch backend — zrb talked through the whole interruption and stopped seconds after the user had finished asking it to. A hold taken for zrb's own voice is given back with `resume_speech`, as before, and a phrase said twice with a wake word in front is no longer dropped as the transcriber guessing at noise — saying stop twice is what someone does when the first one went unheard.
