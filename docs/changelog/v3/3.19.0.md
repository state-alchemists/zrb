## 3.19.0 (October 13, 2026)

- **Feature: the agent can take a photo itself** (`src/zrb/llm/camera/feature.py`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_camera.py`, `docs/llm/voice-camera.md`, `test/llm/camera/test_feature.py`): with `ZRB_LLM_CAMERA_ENABLE_TOOL=on` (default off), `enable_camera` also registers a `TakePhoto` tool that returns the photo to the model. It is tagged `EXECUTE`, so every call asks for approval and plan mode denies it; `auto_approve("TakePhoto")` skips the prompt. A text-only model gets the photo the way it gets a `/photo` attachment: described by `LLM_MULTIMODAL_MODEL`, or, without one, a `[SYSTEM SUGGESTION]` saying it cannot see the photo. `CameraConfig` gains `enable_tool`.
- **Feature: zrb's voice is taken out of what you say over it, on macOS** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/echo_cancel.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_dictation.py`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_voice.py`, `docs/adr/adr-0111.md`, `docs/llm/voice-camera.md`, `test/llm/dictation/test_echo_cancel.py`, `test/llm/dictation/test_listen_microphone.py`): on laptop speakers, talking over zrb transcribed your words and zrb's together. With `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_ENABLE_ECHO_CANCEL=on` (default off; on under `ZRB_LLM_VOICE=conversation`), `listen` opens the default microphone through macOS voice processing (`EchoCancelledMicrophone`), which cancels what the speakers play, so zrb's speech keeps playing in process and only capture changes. Measured on a MacBook's speakers, a clip vosk transcribed nearly whole from the plain microphone left none of its words through voice processing, and a voice talking over it stayed intact. Off macOS, with `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_DEVICE` set, or without `pyobjc-framework-AVFoundation` (now in `zrb[voice]` on macOS), the plain microphone opens and zrb logs why once; voice processing that will not start, or hears nothing within two seconds of starting (as can happen with a Bluetooth headset), is released and falls back to it too, as does a session whose voice processing macOS stops mid-way (the audio devices changed), for the rest of that session. On Linux it is configuration: the voice guide's new "Hearing zrb in what you say" shows how to load PulseAudio's or PipeWire's echo-cancel module. `DictationConfig` gains `enable_echo_cancel`, and `record()` takes an optional `DictationConfig` to record through the same microphone and echo cancellation `listen()` uses.
