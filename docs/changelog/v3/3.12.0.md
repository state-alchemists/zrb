## 3.12.0 (September 30, 2026)

- **Feature: speech reads a reply while it is written** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/streamed_reply.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/chunker.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/feature.py`, ADR-0103): with `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_STREAM`, on by default, `SpeechChunker` cuts the streamed text into cleaned sentences, never inside a code fence or table, and each is spoken as soon as it is complete; text before a tool call is spoken when the call starts. `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_MAX_CHARS` caps what one turn speaks. A turn that streamed nothing is spoken at `Stop` as before, and `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_STREAM=off` restores reading the whole reply at `Stop`.
- **Feature: tool calls announced after a silence** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/progress.py`, ADR-0103): with `progress` in `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_EVENTS`, now in its default, a tool call starting after `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_PROGRESS_INTERVAL` (8) seconds with nothing said is announced from a template ("Running a command."), dropped if the call ends first, and never said while speech plays.
- **Feature: talking over zrb pauses it, then stops it — on laptop speakers too** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/feature.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/player.py`, ADR-0076, ADR-0105): with `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_ENABLED=on`, hands-free keeps listening while zrb speaks; `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_MIN_SPEECH` (0.3 s) of speech over it pauses zrb at once (`pause_speech`), words in the final transcript (or, with wake words, a partial one starting with a wake word) stop it (`interrupt_speech`, muting the rest of the streamed response), and anything else resumes it (`resume_speech`). A stop word said alone (`ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_STOP_WORDS`), over zrb or while a turn runs before zrb speaks, cancels the turn; anything else steers it, or with `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_ACTION=cancel` starts a new one; an answer to a pending approval stays an answer. New `Speaker.pause`/`resume`/`interrupt` (a pause also holds speech queued or being synthesized, not only what is playing; a sentence a player program plays cannot hold, so only it is stopped and `resume` goes on with the next; draining a paused speaker at exit drops what it holds instead of waiting), `UtteranceCutter.is_barge_in`, `Utterance.is_barge_in`, `Utterance.is_over_speech` (words too brief to pause zrb, like a crisp "stop", still stop it and cancel the turn instead of reaching the model as a message), and `listen(on_barge_in=..., on_barge_in_dropped=...)`. The switch is `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_ENABLED`, a boolean (`DictationConfig.barge_in_enabled`, read by `DictationConfig.is_barge_in_enabled`).
- **Feature: barge-in guards against zrb's own voice** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/feature.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/words.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/backend/openai.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/spoken_log.py`, ADR-0105): on speakers the microphone hears zrb, and zrb transcribed its own reply ("Sleep well." heard back as "sleep", then "well") or Whisper made words from noise ("and this and this"), and answered them. What is heard over zrb now passes four guards. A bar over zrb's voice: while it speaks, and for `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_ECHO_COOLDOWN` after, speech must be `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_MARGIN` (3) times the median level zrb's voice reaches the microphone, never below the threshold, so it follows the volume and stays at the threshold on headphones; an utterance keeps the bar it started with. The transcriber's guesses: a Whisper model's segments that its own scores mark as no speech or repetitive are left out (`verbose_json`), and any transcript that is a phrase Whisper writes for silence or one phrase over and over is dropped (`is_transcriber_guess`). zrb's own words: `Speaker` logs each sentence it plays and when (`spoken_log`, `SpokenLog`) — only while it is heard: from when it holds the audio device (`play(on_start=...)`), never for a sentence dropped waiting for it, and not while paused — and a transcript mostly of words zrb was saying then, or for `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_SELF_ECHO_TAIL` (1 s) after, or reading like a stretch of it letter by letter, is dropped (`is_said_back`, `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_SELF_ECHO_MATCH`). At least `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_MIN_WORDS` (2) words over zrb, unless a stop word or an answer to a pending prompt (`count_words`); "wait" and "hold on" join the stop words. A transcript a guard drops leaves zrb carrying on, with a badge saying why (`🎤 ignored "…" (zrb's own voice)`). zrb does not subtract its own voice from the microphone: no echo canceller ships.
- **Feature: speech played in zrb's own process** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/pcm_player.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/audio.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/`, ADR-0103): `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_PLAYER=auto` (default) plays speech through sounddevice when the zrb[voice] extra is installed and the backend renders audio — new `AnySpeechBackend.create_audio(text) -> SpeechAudio | None`, implemented by `say`, `espeak-ng`, `openai` (streamed) and `gemini` — so it can be paused and cancelled out of the microphone; `command` keeps player programs. Audio zrb cannot play safely (not 16-bit mono PCM, a header over 1 MB, a sample rate outside 1–384 kHz) is refused, and the backend's player program speaks it instead; so does a sentence whose output device cannot be opened (none, or busy), or whose audio fails before any of it is heard, rather than being lost. Audio that fails part-way is played as far as it came and logged as cut off. An output device that cannot open is not tried again for the rest of the session, so a cloud voice is not asked for each sentence twice; a stream that opens but fails to start is closed; the play timeout counts playing time only, not a pause; and a configured `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_WAV_PLAYER` plays every sentence, even under `auto`. An unknown `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_PLAYER` is logged and read as `auto`.
- **Feature: hands-free transcribes while you speak** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/backend/any_transcription_stream.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/backend/vosk.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/words.py`, ADR-0076): `AnyDictationBackend.create_stream()` may return an `AnyTranscriptionStream`; vosk's `VoskTranscriptionStream` does. Each utterance is fed to it block by block, the status bar shows the words heard, and the utterance ends after `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_MIN_SILENCE` (0.5 s) once its words sound finished (`is_finished_phrase`) instead of the full `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_SILENCE`. `listen()` takes `create_stream` and `on_partial`; `Utterance.stream` carries the stream. A stream that fails to start or to take audio is logged and given up on for the rest of the listen: utterances are then transcribed whole once they end, and hands-free stays on.
- **Feature: stream observers** (`src/zrb/llm/stream_observer.py`, `src/zrb/llm/agent/run/runner.py`, `src/zrb/llm/task/llm_task.py`, `src/zrb/llm/task/chat/task.py`, ADR-0104): `append_stream_observer` / `prepend_stream_observer` / `remove_stream_observer` plus `set_stream_observers` (which copies the list it is given) on `LLMTask` and `LLMChatTask` register callables seeing every event a run streams, after the UI; `run_agent(stream_observers=...)` passes them each event. One that raises is logged and skipped; a sub-agent's run does not inherit them.
- **Breaking: `AnyUI.cancel_current_turn(reason)`** (`src/zrb/llm/ui/any_ui.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/base/ui.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/multi_ui.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/state_defaults.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/default/keybindings.py`): releases a pending confirmation, cancels the running turn and fires `Stop` with `reason`. Esc now calls it with `"escape"`; a barge-in calls it with `"barge_in"`. `MultiUI` cancels the turn it runs itself (its children run none) and fires `Stop` once; `UIStateDefaultsMixin` ignores it. `AnyUI` also declares `is_waiting_for_answer` and `is_prompt_answered_since(asked_at)`, which `BaseUI` already had and `MultiUI` answers from its children, so dictation and speech no longer probe for them; `UIStateDefaultsMixin` answers `False`. `AnyUI.is_turn_running` says whether a turn runs that `cancel_current_turn` would stop: a child of a `MultiUI` asks its parent, and hands its cancel to it, so Esc in the terminal stops a turn with web or Telegram UIs attached too. The `Stop` that Esc and a barge-in fire goes to the hook manager the turn ran with, not the process-wide one. A class implementing `AnyUI` directly must add all four. New `get_turn_hook_manager(llm_task)` (`src/zrb/llm/ui/turn_hooks.py`) picks the manager a turn's `Stop` goes to.
- **Feature: `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_STYLE` directs how the cloud voices sound** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/openai.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/gemini.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/builtin.py`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_speech.py`, ADR-0103): a plain-words direction sent as OpenAI's `instructions` (not to the `tts-1` models) and ahead of the text in Gemini's prompt, never read aloud. The default asks for a warm, clear, conversational colleague; empty keeps the voice's default manner. `OpenAISpeechBackend` and `GeminiSpeechBackend` take `style`.
- **Fix: emoji no longer overwrite the letter after them in terminals** (`src/zrb/llm/`, `src/zrb/builtin/`, `test/architecture/test_terminal_emoji.py`): emoji made from a narrow symbol by a variation selector (U+FE0F, as in the warning sign, the pencil, the gear) are drawn one column wide by many terminals but counted two by prompt_toolkit, so "✍️ transcribing" read as "trenscribing". Every one in Python source is replaced by an emoji wide by default (warnings ❗, info 🔹, accept-edits mode 📝, normal mode 🔧, a todo in progress 🔄, shell output 💻, and the CLI group and task icons); an architecture test keeps U+FE0F out. The web chat keeps its own, which browsers measure right.
- **Improvement: status badges get a line of their own** (`src/zrb/llm/ui/default/output.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/default/app/layout.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/default/ui.py`): the microphone's state (`🎤 listening`, `✍️ transcribing…`) was drawn inside the status bar, between the working indicator and the token counts, moving with every state change. The default UI now draws the badges on a line above the status bar, shown only while there are any (`UIOutput.get_badge_bar_text`, `create_layout(badge_bar_text=...)`).
- **Performance: the next sentence's audio is made while the current one plays** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/player.py`): `Speaker` runs a synthesis thread and a player thread with at most one utterance waiting ready, so a cloud voice has no gap between sentences.
- **Improvement: the model is told its reply is heard** (`src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/speech_live.md`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/feature.py`): while speech is on, a `speech` live-context provider asks it to open with the answer in a sentence or two and put detail after it.
- **Feature: every voice word, phrase and tuning value is a setting** (`src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_dictation.py`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_speech.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/`, `src/zrb/config/env_field.py`, `test/architecture/test_voice_config_documented.py`, ADR-0076, ADR-0105): read when a session starts, so `zrb_init.py` can change any of them. Dictation: `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_STOP_WORDS` (a list of its own; "no" said alone over zrb no longer cancels a turn when it is not one), `_POLITE_WORDS`, `_TRAILING_WORDS` (the end-of-sentence check for your language), `_BLOCK_DURATION`, `_TURN_END_TIMEOUT`, `_TRANSCRIBE_PROMPT`, `_BARGE_IN_MARGIN`, `_BARGE_IN_MIN_WORDS`, `_SELF_ECHO_MATCH` and `_SELF_ECHO_TAIL`. Speech: `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_PROGRESS_PHRASES` and `_APPROVAL_ACTIONS` (JSON objects of tool-name patterns to what is said, `{tool}` filled in; a new `json_object` config type), `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_QUESTION_MESSAGE`, `_APPROVAL_MESSAGE`, `_APPROVAL_TARGET_KEYS`, `_APPROVAL_TARGET_MAX_CHARS`, `_PROGRESS_SILENT_TOOLS`, `_GEMINI_PROMPT`, `_GEMINI_STYLE_PROMPT`, `_RENDER_TIMEOUT`, `_STALL_TIMEOUT`, `_PLAYER_BLOCK_FRAMES`, `_PLAYER_READ_AHEAD`. New `is_said_alone`; the four Vosk archive limits are now documented. A test fails when a voice setting has no config field or no row in `llm-config.md`. `listen.BLOCK_SECONDS` is gone: the block is `DictationConfig.block_duration`.
- **Feature: `ZRB_LLM_VOICE` sets how a session talks** (`src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_voice.py`, `docs/configuration/llm-config.md`): `off` (default), `speak` (replies read aloud), `turns` (read aloud and always listening) or `conversation` (as `turns`, with barge-in). It moves the defaults of `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_ENABLED`, `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_MODE` and `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_ENABLED`; each set on its own still wins. An unknown preset raises a `ValueError` naming the valid ones.
- **Improvement: mistyped and retired settings are named at startup** (`src/zrb/__main__.py`, `src/zrb/config/config.py`, `src/zrb/config/retired.py`, ADR-0026): a set `ZRB_*` variable that nearly matches a setting (`ZRB_LLM_MODELL`) is reported with the setting it meant, and one zrb no longer reads (the `ZRB_LLM_VOICE_*` settings 3.10.0 retired) with what replaced it. A project's own `ZRB_*` variables, matching no setting closely, stay quiet. New `Config.get_mistyped_env_keys`, `Config.get_retired_env_keys`, `EnvField.get_read_keys`, and a `cutoff` for `suggest_name`.
- **Improvement: a removed `zrb` export says what to use instead** (`src/zrb/__init__.py`): `from zrb import llm_config` (or `LLMConfig`, `UICommands`, `fstring`, removed in 3.0.0) raises an `ImportError` naming the replacement, not a bare "cannot import name".
- **Improvement: a missing OpenAI key names `ZRB_LLM_API_KEY` too** (`src/zrb/llm/agent/run/error_classifier.py`): the OpenAI SDK's own "Missing credentials" error, raised for `openai:` and OpenAI-compatible models, now gets the same hint as pydantic-ai's.
- **Improvement: swallowed broad exceptions and `Any` annotations are ratcheted** (`test/architecture/test_broad_except_ratchet.py`, `test/architecture/test_any_annotation_ratchet.py`): 324 `except Exception:` handlers that do not re-raise and 1,097 `Any` annotations; both counts may only go down.
- **Performance: PyYAML loads only for a YAML hook file** (`src/zrb/llm/hook/manager_loading.py`), about 20 ms off every start.
- **Fix: a turn cancelled with Esc is no longer read aloud at `Stop`** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/feature.py`): a `Stop` whose payload names a `reason` is treated as cancelled.
- **Fix: a configured phrase holding a placeholder is not filled twice** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/text.py`): `fill_template` substitutes in one pass, so a `{text}` inside `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_STYLE` stays as written.
- **Improvement: the spoken-reply prompt no longer reads as asking for an "On screen:" heading** (`src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/speech_live.md`).
