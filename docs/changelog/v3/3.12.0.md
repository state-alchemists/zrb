## 3.12.0 (September 30, 2026)

- **Feature: speech reads a reply while it is written** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/streamed_reply.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/chunker.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/feature.py`, ADR-0103): with `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_STREAM`, on by default, `SpeechChunker` cuts the streamed text into cleaned sentences, never inside a code fence or table, and each is spoken as soon as it is complete; text before a tool call is spoken when the call starts. `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_MAX_CHARS` caps what one turn speaks. A turn that streamed nothing is spoken at `Stop` as before, and `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_STREAM=off` restores reading the whole reply at `Stop`.
- **Feature: tool calls announced after a silence** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/progress.py`, ADR-0103): with `progress` in `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_EVENTS`, now in its default, a tool call starting after `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_PROGRESS_INTERVAL` (8) seconds with nothing said is announced from a template ("Running a command."), dropped if the call ends first, and never said while speech plays.
- **Feature: talking over zrb pauses it, then stops it — on laptop speakers too** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/feature.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/echo/`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/player.py`, ADR-0076, ADR-0105): with `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN=on`, hands-free keeps listening while zrb speaks; `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_MIN_SPEECH` (0.3 s) of speech over it pauses zrb at once (`pause_speech`), words in the partial or final transcript stop it (`interrupt_speech`, muting the rest of the streamed response), and anything else resumes it (`resume_speech`). A stop word said alone (`ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_STOP_WORDS`) cancels the turn; anything else steers it, or with `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_BARGE_IN_ACTION=cancel` starts a new one; an answer to a pending approval stays an answer. New `Speaker.pause`/`resume`/`interrupt` (a pause also holds speech queued or being synthesized, not only what is playing), `UtteranceCutter.is_barge_in`, `Utterance.is_barge_in`, `Utterance.is_over_speech` (words too brief to pause zrb, like a crisp "stop", still stop it and cancel the turn instead of reaching the model as a message), and `listen(on_barge_in=..., on_barge_in_dropped=..., echo=...)`.
- **Feature: echo cancellation for barge-in** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/echo/`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/echo_reference.py`, ADR-0105): `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_ECHO_CANCELLER` picks how zrb's voice is removed from the microphone: `numpy` (default), an adaptive filter in pure NumPy, or `none` (trust the microphone). `EchoCancellation` measures the echo delay from the audio (GCC-PHAT), stamps both sides with the host's DAC/ADC times, and keeps the microphone deaf over zrb until what is left of its voice cannot start an utterance. `AnyEchoCanceller` is the interface for another canceller.
- **Feature: speech played in zrb's own process** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/pcm_player.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/audio.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/`, ADR-0103): `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_PLAYER=auto` (default) plays speech through sounddevice when the zrb[voice] extra is installed and the backend renders audio — new `AnySpeechBackend.create_audio(text) -> SpeechAudio | None`, implemented by `say`, `espeak-ng`, `openai` (streamed) and `gemini` — so it can be paused and cancelled out of the microphone; `command` keeps player programs.
- **Feature: hands-free transcribes while you speak** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/backend/any_transcription_stream.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/backend/vosk.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/words.py`, ADR-0076): `AnyDictationBackend.create_stream()` may return an `AnyTranscriptionStream`; vosk's `VoskTranscriptionStream` does. Each utterance is fed to it block by block, the status bar shows the words heard, and the utterance ends after `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_MIN_SILENCE` (0.5 s) once its words sound finished (`is_finished_phrase`) instead of the full `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_SILENCE`. `listen()` takes `create_stream` and `on_partial`; `Utterance.stream` carries the stream.
- **Feature: stream observers** (`src/zrb/llm/stream_observer.py`, `src/zrb/llm/agent/run/runner.py`, `src/zrb/llm/task/llm_task.py`, `src/zrb/llm/task/chat/task.py`, ADR-0104): `append_stream_observer` / `prepend_stream_observer` / `remove_stream_observer` on `LLMTask` and `LLMChatTask` (plus `set_stream_observers` on the latter) register callables seeing every event a run streams, after the UI; `run_agent(stream_observers=...)` passes them each event. One that raises is logged and skipped; a sub-agent's run does not inherit them.
- **Breaking: `AnyUI.cancel_current_turn(reason)`** (`src/zrb/llm/ui/any_ui.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/base/ui.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/multi_ui.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/state_defaults.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/default/keybindings.py`): releases a pending confirmation, cancels the running turn and fires `Stop` with `reason`. Esc now calls it with `"escape"`; a barge-in calls it with `"barge_in"`. `MultiUI` forwards it; `UIStateDefaultsMixin` ignores it. A class implementing `AnyUI` directly must add it.
- **Feature: `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_STYLE` directs how the cloud voices sound** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/openai.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/gemini.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/backend/builtin.py`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_speech.py`, ADR-0103): a plain-words direction sent as OpenAI's `instructions` (not to the `tts-1` models) and ahead of the text in Gemini's prompt, never read aloud. The default asks for a warm, clear, conversational colleague; empty keeps the voice's default manner. `OpenAISpeechBackend` and `GeminiSpeechBackend` take `style`.
- **Fix: emoji no longer overwrite the letter after them in terminals** (`src/zrb/llm/`, `src/zrb/builtin/`, `test/architecture/test_terminal_emoji.py`): emoji made from a narrow symbol by a variation selector (U+FE0F, as in the warning sign, the pencil, the gear) are drawn one column wide by many terminals but counted two by prompt_toolkit, so "✍️ transcribing" read as "trenscribing". Every one in Python source is replaced by an emoji wide by default (warnings ❗, info 🔹, accept-edits mode 📝, normal mode 🔧, a todo in progress 🔄, shell output 💻, and the CLI group and task icons); an architecture test keeps U+FE0F out. The web chat keeps its own, which browsers measure right.
- **Improvement: status badges get a line of their own** (`src/zrb/llm/ui/default/output.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/default/app/layout.py`, `src/zrb/llm/ui/default/ui.py`): the microphone's state (`🎤 listening`, `✍️ transcribing…`) was drawn inside the status bar, between the working indicator and the token counts, moving with every state change. The default UI now draws the badges on a line above the status bar, shown only while there are any (`UIOutput.get_badge_bar_text`, `create_layout(badge_bar_text=...)`).
- **Performance: the next sentence's audio is made while the current one plays** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/player.py`): `Speaker` runs a synthesis thread and a player thread with at most one utterance waiting ready, so a cloud voice has no gap between sentences.
- **Improvement: the model is told its reply is heard** (`src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/speech_live.md`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/feature.py`): while speech is on, a `speech` live-context provider asks it to open with the answer in a sentence or two and put detail after it.
- **Feature: every voice word, phrase and tuning value is a setting** (`src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_dictation.py`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_speech.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/`, `src/zrb/config/env_field.py`, `test/architecture/test_voice_config_documented.py`, ADR-0076, ADR-0105): read when a session starts, so `zrb_init.py` can change any of them. Dictation: `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_STOP_WORDS` (a list of its own; "no" said alone over zrb no longer cancels a turn when it is not one), `_POLITE_WORDS`, `_TRAILING_WORDS` (the end-of-sentence check for your language), `_BLOCK_DURATION`, `_TURN_END_TIMEOUT`, `_TRANSCRIBE_PROMPT`, and eighteen `_ECHO_*` echo cancellation knobs. Speech: `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_PROGRESS_PHRASES` and `_APPROVAL_ACTIONS` (JSON objects of tool-name patterns to what is said, `{tool}` filled in; a new `json_object` config type), `ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_QUESTION_MESSAGE`, `_APPROVAL_MESSAGE`, `_APPROVAL_TARGET_KEYS`, `_APPROVAL_TARGET_MAX_CHARS`, `_PROGRESS_SILENT_TOOLS`, `_GEMINI_PROMPT`, `_GEMINI_STYLE_PROMPT`, `_RENDER_TIMEOUT`, `_STALL_TIMEOUT`, `_PLAYER_BLOCK_FRAMES`, `_PLAYER_READ_AHEAD`. `EchoCancellation` and `NumpyEchoCanceller` take a `DictationConfig` instead of keyword tuning; `get_echo_canceller(name, config)`; new `is_said_alone`; the four Vosk archive limits are now documented. A test fails when a voice setting has no config field or no row in `llm-config.md`. `listen.BLOCK_SECONDS` is gone: the block is `DictationConfig.block_duration`.
- **Fix: a turn cancelled with Esc is no longer read aloud at `Stop`** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/feature.py`): a `Stop` whose payload names a `reason` is treated as cancelled.
