## 3.23.2 (Unreleased)

- **Fix: a sentence longer than the player's read-ahead was cut off** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/pcm_player.py`, `test/llm/speech/test_pcm_player.py`): `PcmUtterance` stopped reading a sentence's audio as soon as its read-ahead buffer (`ZRB_LLM_SPEECH_PLAYER_READ_AHEAD`, default 32 chunks) stayed full for 5 ms, so anything past the buffer was never played. A service that renders faster than realtime, such as Pocket TTS (about 2.5 s of audio at the default), lost the tail of every longer sentence. The reader now waits for room, so each sentence plays in full. Present since 3.23.1's stutter fix.
- **Fix: a speech service that died kept the fallback voice for the rest of the session** (`src/zrb/llm/speech/player.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/feature.py`, `test/llm/speech/test_player_in_process.py`, `test/llm/speech/test_feature.py`): once Pipecat marked a service unusable, `Speaker` kept its cached pipeline, so every later sentence was spoken by the player-program voice, and `/speech` off and on did not help. `Speaker.restart_backends()` now drops the cached backends after 3 failed in-process renders in a row and when speech is switched back on, so the next sentence builds a fresh service.
