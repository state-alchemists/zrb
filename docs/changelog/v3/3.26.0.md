## 3.26.0 (October 23, 2026)

- **Feature: compaction renders the kept tail to text instead of resending it as structured messages, so the split no longer has to dodge a tool call/return pair** (`src/zrb/llm/summarizer/history_splitter.py`, `src/zrb/llm/summarizer/history_summarizer.py`, `src/zrb/llm/message.py`): a pair spanning nearly the whole history (a call near the start, its return near the end) could leave no split point both within the tail budget and safe for the pair, so the fallback search degenerated into summarizing the entire history as one chunk. The kept tail is now rendered with the same lossless `message_to_text` conversion used to prepare a summarized chunk, folded into the compaction turn as its own section after the `<state_snapshot>` and uncapped by `LLM_SUMMARY_MAX_TOKEN_RATIO`, so the split is a plain token-budget cut — `find_safe_split_index`, `find_best_effort_split` and `is_split_safe` are gone. A tool call still awaiting its result (a pending deferred call) is the one exception: `pending_tail_start` finds it and the kept tail is widened to cover it, so it still reaches the model as a real `ToolCallPart`, resumable as before. A tail that fails even the tight 40% budget now gets a second attempt at a looser 80% (`FALLBACK_BUDGET_RATIO`) before falling back to keeping nothing ([ADR-0041](../../adr/adr-0041.md), revised).

- **Fix: a summary's size bound is a share of the threshold, not 2000 tokens** (`src/zrb/llm/summarizer/history_summarizer.py`, `src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_content.py`, `src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/conversational_summarizer.md`): `LLM_SUMMARY_MAX_TOKENS` (2000) was measured on the whole inserted block — zrb's framing, the journal index and the archive pointer — while the summarizer prompt asked the model to keep the `<state_snapshot>` under 2000 on its own, so a compliant summary was over the cap by construction: shortened twice, then truncated mid-word, with `<task_state>` — last in the schema — the first thing lost. The same 2000 was under 1% of what a compaction frees on a 500,000-token window, and about a quarter of a small model's. It is now `LLM_SUMMARY_MAX_TOKEN_RATIO` (0.05 of the trigger threshold — 18,750 tokens on that window — with a 1,000-token floor), so the bound follows the model instead of meaning something different on every one. The cut is schema-aware as well: whole sections go from the least durable end first and the closing tag survives, instead of a mid-word truncation that took `<task_state>` first. `LLM_SUMMARY_MAX_TOKENS` is retired and reported at startup, and the summarizer prompt asks for density rather than a token count ([ADR-0041](../../adr/adr-0041.md), revised).
