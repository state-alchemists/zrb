## 3.16.1 (October 11, 2026)

- **Fix: dictation recovers when PortAudio stops the microphone** (`src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `test/llm/dictation/test_listen.py`): an inactive microphone stream is closed and reopened instead of leaving listening stalled. Audio lost while the stream was down starts a new boundary, so speech captured before and after the failure is not joined into one utterance.
