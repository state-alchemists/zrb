## 3.40.1 (November 16, 2026)

- **Improvement: a where-question is answered with a model, not a tour** (`src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/workflow.md`): asked where something lives, an agent listed files, because nothing in the prompt said what understanding wants. The workflow section now names the shape — the part that owns the answer, the two or three places that decide it, which steps are the user's policy and which are code, one screen, one layer short — and the test of a model: the reader can say what happens when something changes. Where the evidence does not support a coherent model, it says what is missing instead of inventing one. The rule is written for any system, not only zrb, and it pays for itself: the paragraph above it about waiting is one sentence now.
- **Documentation: the whole shape is on the first screen of the architecture section** (`docs/architecture/README.md`): a reader entering the section had to cross three pages before learning what the boxes were, and the six-box map kept outside this repository cannot be linked from a page a clone can read. The section now opens with *In one breath* — the six boxes, the five LLM layers, and the one rule that makes a file's imports say which layer it belongs to.
