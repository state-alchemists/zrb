## 3.41.2 (November 18, 2026)

- **Fix: the reviewer is told the hour, so a diary paragraph stops inventing one** (`src/zrb/llm/hook/builtin/memory_reviewer.py`, `src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/memory_reviewer.md`): the note handed to the reviewer carried the day's date and how much room its page had, and no clock — while a paragraph is written about a moment that has already passed. With no hour to check, it named one from the shape of the turn, and on 2026-11-18 three paragraphs about events between six and seven in the morning said "late in the morning", "late in the evening" and "late in the night". The note now reads ``Today is `2026-11-18` and the clock reads 06:56``, and the prompt says the clock is the hour the review runs rather than the hour the moment happened, so a turn that carries no time gets "then" and nothing more. The note grows by about six tokens a turn.
