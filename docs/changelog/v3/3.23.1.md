## 3.23.1 (October 20, 2026)

- **Improvement: dictation follows a change in the audio devices, and speech no longer stutters at its start** (`src/zrb/llm/util/audio_devices.py`, `src/zrb/llm/dictation/listen.py`, `src/zrb/llm/speech/pcm_player.py`, ADR-0114): PortAudio has no hotplug callback, so hands-free listening now compares the device list and the default microphone/speaker pair every 2 seconds and, on a change (a headset plugged in, taken off, or made default), reopens the microphone without splicing an utterance across the gap. The watch is off for a microphone named in `ZRB_LLM_DICTATION_DEVICE` and for the echo-cancelled one. Playback starts its output stream once 0.1 s of audio stands by, so the first block is sound, not silence; a late block counts as an underrun and is said once; and an output stream PortAudio stops on its own ends the sentence instead of the player waiting out its timeout.
