## 3.41.1 (November 17, 2026)

- **Improvement: the reviewer writes a decision, not a report** (`src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/memory_reviewer.md`): a claim was "what is true", so the injected block read as a pile of statements written by someone else, and the reason behind each one was pushed into `body`, which is never injected. A claim is now one sentence carrying what is true, or what was decided, and the reason the turn gave for it — in the first person when the decision is the assistant's, and plainly when it is a fact about the project or a preference the user stated, because a fact narrated as `I` is a lie in that voice. A turn that already called `Remember` or `Forget` is now the priority item rather than a second claim: the reviewer searches that subject and revises its id instead of filing a second answer beside it, which is how one subject came to hold two files. Its sentence is kept where it already says what is true, and the reviewer may still answer `skip` when the flagged turn shows nothing durable.
- **Fix: a full day is handed to the reviewer, not refused silently** (`src/zrb/llm/hook/builtin/memory_reviewer.py`, `src/zrb/llm/agent/hook_agent.py`): a diary is written one paragraph per turn into a page capped at 8,000 characters, and the reviewer holds one turn's transcript, never the page. Once the day filled, every later append came back refused to a reviewer that could not tell a full page from a day that had not changed — on 2026-11-17 the body reached 7,962 characters at half past ten in the morning and recorded nothing for the rest of the day. When less than 600 characters of room remain, the hook now hands the reviewer the day's body along with the turn and asks for the whole day back, shorter, keeping every quoted moment and the reason behind a decision. `AgentHookConfig` gained `input_note` for it: the note is a function of the context, because a config is built once and outlives the day it describes.
- **Tests: the hang test's budget outlasts its own start-up** (`test/llm/screenshot/backend/test_tool.py`): the capture budget was 50ms, and a loaded Windows runner spent it creating the temporary directory before the program was spawned, so the test failed on "no time left for this capture" — a real branch, but not the timeout it was asserting. One second leaves room for the start-up overhead while staying far under the five-second hang, and the spent-deadline branch keeps its own test.
