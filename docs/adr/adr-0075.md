# ADR-0075 — A `closing` section re-anchors the end-of-turn rules as the prompt's last words

**Status:** Accepted

**Context.** Instruction-following under a primacy bias drops later rules
first (ADR-0045), but the *recency* end of the prompt is the boundary the model
crosses into its own reply: the last instructions read are the best-recalled
ones, and the ones that matter at that boundary are the two that decide what
leaves the turn — verify the artifact, and close (report what changed, stop when
asked). Those two rules lived buried in `workflow`'s Verify Before Done and
Recovery sections, mid-prompt, with `examples`, `system_context` and
`project_context` between them and the end. Nothing re-stated them at the point
of maximum recall.

**Decision.** Add a `closing` section, last in `DEFAULT_LLM_INCLUDE_SECTIONS`
(`persona,workflow,examples,system_context,project_context,closing`). It is a
compact, file-backed section that **restates** the two end-of-turn rules in one
sentence each. It adds no rule of its own — every word restates behavior
`workflow` already owns, which is what keeps it MECE with the rest of the
prompt. Being file-backed, it inherits the full override chain
(project override → env → base prompt dir → package) and the profile-variant
fallback like any other section; a trimmed config simply drops it, and a
project that pins its own section list opts in by naming `closing`.

Placing a *new* rule here would fail the "a rule lives where it is enforced"
ladder (ADR-0045) — the section is deliberately barred from owning behavior.
Its position is its whole point: the last thing the model reads.

**Consequences.** The prompt gains ~200 chars and one section name; the
end-of-turn behaviors are re-stated at the highest-recall position. Pinned
configs are unaffected until they name `closing`. The subset walk
(`test_section_composition.py`) treats it as a file section, so the section
must read whole on its own and must not use vocabulary owned by an absent
section.

**Alternatives rejected.** *Append the anchor to the end of `workflow.md`* —
still mid-prompt, followed by three sections of weaker recall value. *Fuse it
into `system_context`'s Python* — same mid-prompt problem (it precedes
`project_context`) and couples a prose anchor to a runtime-facts module.
*Enforce it in code* — a runtime hook cannot decide whether an artifact was
verified; this is judgment, so it belongs in the prompt.

**Where it lives.** `src/zrb/llm/prompt/markdown/closing.md`,
`src/zrb/config/mixins/llm_prompt.py`
(`DEFAULT_LLM_INCLUDE_SECTIONS`), `test/llm/prompt/test_config_defaults.py`,
`test/llm/prompt/test_section_composition.py`.
